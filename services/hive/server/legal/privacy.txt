NEXUS-WAY CONNECT — PRIVACY POLICY (Beta)
Last updated: 2026-07-31

Plain-English summary: this service collects almost nothing, shows no ads,
runs no analytics, and cannot read your end-to-end-encrypted content. It is
offered in the United States only.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT
   - Your handle and optional display name / bio / avatar. Your HANDLE and
     these profile fields are chosen by you and are PUBLIC — anything you
     put in them (including a real name, if you choose) is public by your
     own choice.
   - Your public cryptographic keys (never private keys).
   - Content you post (posts, comments, reactions, media), stored so it
     can be delivered to the audience you chose.
   - Device records: a name you choose, the device's public key, its IP
     address and last-seen time (deleted after 90 days of inactivity).
   - A boolean attestation that you are 18 or older. We do NOT collect
     your birth date, real name, email, or phone number.
   - An operational audit log (sign-ins, moderation actions). Routine
     entries are deleted after 90 days; moderation records are retained.
   - If you set a recovery password: an encrypted recovery blob that the
     server cannot decrypt.
    - Support conversations you choose to send for bug reports, feature
       requests, help, or other requests. These are linked to your account and
       readable by authorized Console operators so they can reply.

2. WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT
   No email address. No phone number. No real name. No location data.
   No advertising identifiers. No analytics or tracking of any kind.

3. HOW YOUR DATA IS USED
   Solely to operate the service: authenticating your devices, delivering
   your content to your chosen audience, responding to support requests, and
   moderating reported content.
   Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used to train AI.

3A. SERVICE PROVIDERS
   During the beta we route traffic through Cloudflare, which operates the
   network path between your app and our server and therefore processes
   connection metadata, including your IP address, on our behalf. Cloudflare
   is our only third-party service provider; we use no analytics or
   advertising services. If you subscribe after the beta, a payment provider
   will process your payment and hold your billing details — we will not.

4. WHO CAN SEE YOUR CONTENT
   Exactly the audience you chose: public, approved followers, or the
   members of a Fold (a member-only group). IMPORTANT — what is and isn't
   encrypted from us:
   - PUBLIC posts and profiles are stored as plain text on the server, so
     that strangers can read them. These are NOT hidden from the operator.
    - Fold content, direct messages, and your Vault data are
     end-to-end encrypted: the server stores only ciphertext and cannot
     read them. (A small amount of Fold content posted during the early
     beta, before Fold encryption shipped, may still be stored unencrypted
     until it is deleted or migrated.)
    - Voice and video calls: the server relays only call setup metadata
       (which account called which, and when) so devices can find each
       other. The call itself is encrypted directly between the two
       devices and is never stored. If a relay server is needed to
       connect the call, it carries only that encrypted traffic.
    - To synchronize retained direct-message history across your devices, each
       source device encrypts a separate bounded history snapshot to the target
       device. The server stores only target-scoped ciphertext for up to 30 days
       and cannot read the snapshot.
    - Support conversations are NOT end-to-end encrypted. They are
       TLS-protected in transit and stored so authorized Console operators can
       read and answer them.
    - When you report encrypted Fold content, the app explicitly submits your
       decrypted text copy as reporter-supplied moderation evidence. Fold media
       remains encrypted unless you separately submit a plaintext evidence copy.
   Discoverability in search is off unless you turn it on. Blocking hides
   you and them from each other.

5. RETENTION AND DELETION
   - Deleting a post or comment deletes it (media storage is reclaimed).
   - Deleting your account removes your content and profile from the
     server. Moderation/legal audit records are retained as required.
   - Routine logs: 90 days. Device IPs: cleared after 90 days inactive.
   - When content is reported, we preserve a frozen snapshot of the
     reported content and its associated account records (including IP
     records) as a moderation record for as long as the report and any
     resulting enforcement remain relevant.
    - Support conversations remain with your account until account deletion;
       reply/status audit records may be retained as operational records.
   - EXCEPTION for illegal activity: if your account is used for activity
     that is illegal under U.S. law or that seriously violates the Terms
     of Service, you forfeit these deletion and minimization protections
     for that account. We may then freeze and preserve its entire
     footprint — posts, comments, media, profile, follow/block
     relationships, and device, session, and IP history — outside the
     normal 90-day window, and your deletions become preserved tombstones
     rather than erasure. See Section 6 of the Terms of Service.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
   From the app you can: export all your data (machine-readable), correct
   your posts and profile, and delete your account. These work for
   everyone, not just residents of specific states.

7. SECURITY
   All connections use TLS. Authentication is by cryptographic signature;
   the server never sees a password for sign-in. Session tokens are
   stored hashed. Recovery data is encrypted on your device before upload.
   Recovery is OPTIONAL and your responsibility: if you do not set a
   recovery password and lose all your devices, your account cannot be
   recovered by anyone, including the operator. See the Terms of Service.

8. DISCLOSURE
   Data is disclosed only if legally compelled (e.g., a valid court
   order), or where an account has forfeited its protections through
   illegal activity (Terms of Service Section 6), and only what exists —
   which never includes your private keys. In those cases we may preserve
   and disclose the affected account's data (including device, session,
   and IP history) to law enforcement and other lawful authorities.
   Suspected CSAM is reported to NCMEC as required by federal law
   (18 U.S.C. §2258A).

9. BREACH NOTIFICATION
   If a breach affecting your data is discovered, affected users will be
   notified through the service without unreasonable delay.

10. CONTACT
   Use the Support action in Connect Messages, or contact the operator
   directly, for privacy questions or requests.
