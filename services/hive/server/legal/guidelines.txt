NEXUS-WAY CONNECT — COMMUNITY GUIDELINES
Last updated: 2026-07-31

Connect is a small, ad-free, chronological space. There is no algorithm
deciding what you see — just the people you follow, in time order. These
Guidelines are part of the Terms of Service. They explain what is and isn't
allowed, and how we handle it. Keep it decent and keep it legal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHO CAN BE HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- You must be 18 or older.
- During the beta, access is by invitation only.
- Connect is offered in the United States only.
If we learn an account belongs to someone under 18, we remove it. A parent or
guardian who cannot remove a minor's account themselves can contact the
operator (Section 8) to have it removed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT'S NOT ALLOWED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not post, share, store, or send:

  ILLEGAL CONTENT
  - Anything illegal under U.S. law.
  - CHILD SEXUAL ABUSE MATERIAL (CSAM) or any sexual content involving
    minors, real or simulated. Zero tolerance. See Section 6.

  HARM TO PEOPLE
  - Harassment, bullying, stalking, or targeted abuse.
  - Credible threats of violence, or incitement of violence.
  - Content that promotes terrorism or facilitates serious crime.
  - Encouraging suicide or self-harm.

  PRIVACY VIOLATIONS
  - Other people's private or identifying information without their
    consent ("doxxing").
  - Non-consensual intimate imagery.

  DECEPTION & ABUSE OF THE PLATFORM
  - Impersonating another person or entity to deceive.
  - Spam, scams, or bulk unsolicited messaging.
  - Malware, or attempts to break, overload, or bypass the security of the
    service or other people's accounts.

  INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
  - Content that infringes someone else's copyright or trademark. See
    Section 7 for how copyright complaints work.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. YOUR CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
You are not at the mercy of an algorithm here. You can:
  - Choose each post's audience: public, followers, or a member-only "Fold."
  - Approve or decline followers.
  - Block anyone — a block hides you and them from each other, both ways.
  - Control who may comment on your posts.
Use these first. Blocking is often the fastest fix for someone you simply
don't want to interact with.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Anyone can report a post, comment, or account, with a reason. Reports go to
the operator for review. Reporting is not a popularity vote — content is
judged against these Guidelines, not by how many reports it gets. Please
don't file false or retaliatory reports; abusing the report system is itself
a violation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HOW ENFORCEMENT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
During the beta the operator personally reviews reports. Depending on
severity, we may:
  - leave the content up (no violation);
  - remove or hide the content;
  - warn the account;
  - suspend the account; or
  - permanently terminate the account.
Serious content — for example, anything reported as CSAM — may be hidden
immediately, before review, to protect the community.

When an account is used for ILLEGAL activity, it forfeits the normal privacy
and deletion protections: its content and account data (including device and
IP history) are preserved and may be turned over to law enforcement, as
described in the Terms of Service (Illegal Activity section). You cannot
delete your way out of an active preservation hold.

Moderation actions are logged. Response times during the beta are
best-effort — it's a very small operation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. CHILD SAFETY (CSAM) — ZERO TOLERANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CSAM and any sexualization of minors are absolutely prohibited. When we
become aware of such content, we:
  - immediately hide it from everyone;
  - preserve it and the associated account data as required by law; and
  - report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children
    (NCMEC), as required by 18 U.S.C. §2258A.
We do not "investigate" or circulate such material; we preserve and report
it. Accounts involved are terminated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. COPYRIGHT (DMCA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Connect respects intellectual-property rights and expects you to as well.
  - If you believe content on Connect infringes your copyright, send a notice
    to the operator (Section 8) identifying the work and the content at issue,
    with a statement of good-faith belief and your contact information.
  - We will review valid notices and may hide the content pending resolution.
  - REPEAT INFRINGERS: accounts that repeatedly infringe are subject to a
    three-strike policy, with account termination on the third strike.
  - COUNTER-NOTICE: if you believe your content was removed by mistake or
    misidentification, you may submit a counter-notice. If you do, the content
    may be restored unless the complainant pursues the matter further, as
    provided under the DMCA.
(The formal designated-agent contact and full notice/counter-notice procedure
will be published before public launch.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. APPEALS & CONTACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you believe a moderation decision was wrong, you can appeal by contacting
the operator directly. During the beta this is handled personally and
out-of-band. Parents/guardians and copyright holders can use the same contact
channel. (A published contact address will accompany the public launch.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Be a real person. Don't hurt people. Don't break the law. Don't post other
people's private stuff or someone else's work as your own. Use blocks and
audience controls freely. If something's wrong, report it. That's it.
